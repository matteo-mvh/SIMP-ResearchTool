Numerical interpretation

Only sampled, strictly positive initial states are shown. Equilibrium assignments require persistent proximity to a locally stable equilibrium; cycle assignments require proximity to a verified periodic orbit and recurrence over three periods. Grey means unresolved, not absence of an attractor. The integration horizon and finite sampling limit the basin map; the points do not establish exact separatrices or basin volumes. Zero-biomass faces are not sampled. Changing the slider selects a separate autonomous system, not a time-dependent simulation.
